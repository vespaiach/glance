Global

Layout
  • Side navigation hides itself after 15 s with no touch or key press: an open drawer closes and the rail slides away, leaving the “Show sidebar” grip. It stays while a sheet, the keyboard or a text field is active, and never hides when Settings › Hide sidebar when idle is off. Tapping the grip brings it back and restarts the 15 s. While the grip shows, every view, Today included, keeps a 36 px strip on that edge (24 px on small) so the grip never covers content.
  • Fluid web app, not a scaled 1920 canvas. Three screen sizes by window width: large (wider than 1200 px), normal (wider than 768 px, up to and including 1200 px) and small (768 px and below, the phone layout). Normal uses the large layout for now; anything below that says “large” applies to normal too. iPad portrait (768–834 px) splits: 768 is small, wider is normal. Layout re-evaluates live on resize.
  • Large fits one screen; on Today the three columns are always the viewport height minus the gutters, so the page never scrolls vertically; small stacks into a single scrolling column.
  • Today uses the Now panel layout. Large: three columns of equal height that fit the viewport (no minimum height, no page scrollbar), widths 0.8 / 1.8 / 0.95 fr (min 280 / 300 / 280 px; at 1920 about 404 / 909 / 480 px), Now panel (clock, weather now, current or next event) in the left column at full height, schedule in the middle, notes over Today’s photo in the right column. Normal: two columns (0.8 / 1.2 fr, min 280 / 300 px) in two rows, Now panel and schedule on the first row, notes and Today’s photo on the second. The first row is at least the viewport height minus the top gutter minus 90 px (min 500 px), so on a short day the top of the second row shows at the bottom, and it grows with the schedule: the whole timeline shows with no scrollbar inside the card, and only the page scrolls. The Now panel stretches to the row’s height; its content (clock, weather, event) sticks 1 gutter below the top of the screen while the schedule scrolls past and stops at the panel’s bottom edge, so it never covers notes. Today’s photo is as tall as its content (4:3 photo frame, capped at 25% of the viewport height minus 24 px on large; 50% minus 64 px on normal), so it never changes size with the note count; the notes panel takes the rest of the column, its “{n} more notes” row pinned to the bottom and its empty tile filling the panel. Notes stack in one column, one per row; large shows 2 notes when both fit above Today’s photo, otherwise 1; normal shows up to 2 (notes sit beside Today’s photo, so there is no height check); a “{n} more notes” row opens the rest. Small: one scrolling column, Now panel, schedule, notes, Today’s photo. Today’s photo (was “Picture of the day”) has its own white panel: eyebrow, “Add photo”, the photo (tap opens it in Photos), its title, a mono caption and, when there are other photos, a “{n} more photos” row. Full spec: Today › Today’s photo. The schedule header shows “Add event” and “Full calendar” as text buttons from 1440 px; below that they are icon buttons (calendar-plus, calendar) so they stay on the header row. Each add button has its own icon so the Today headers never show three identical +: calendar-plus (event), square-pen (note), image-plus (photo); Calendar “Add event” and the Notes view “Add note” use the same icons. The schedule fills its card’s height. The clock and temperature scale with the panel’s width. Body type scales up past 1920×1080, up to 1.5×.
  • Small (reference 390×844): one column everywhere. Today: compact Now panel with no clock, then the schedule as a day list (see Today). Hourly and 7-day forecast scroll sideways. Calendar: a compact week strip card, then the day list in a white card (see Calendar › Header and Day list), on the same canvas, 24 px gutters and cards as every other small screen. Weather: the four details (wind, humidity, UV index, sunset) sit in a 2 × 2 grid inside the weather block instead of one row of four. Photo and background thumbs go to 2 columns. Nav: all seven buttons (44 px) spread edge to edge across the bar with no brand slot, so Settings stays on screen at 390 px. The offline pill sits above the bar.
  • Photo background: every zone with text sits on a white panel (radius 24), so every view reads on a photo.
  • Canvas: the season-color background (soft gray-tinted) sits behind white panels, the reverse of the design system's white gutters. Deliberate, so one layout works on both the season color and a photo.
  • Type: smaller than the design system's 1920 kiosk scale so the app fits laptops and tablets on the large layout. Clock and temperature sizes are set per view (see Today › Now panel and Ambient). Mono captions are 14 px minimum (16 px on Ambient). Text is black on light surfaces and white on black or deep surfaces; no gray or opacity-dimmed text. The only exception is Ambient’s Medium and Low brightness (solid light grays).
  • Spacing follows the 8 px scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32).
  • Today spacing: 24 px between every box on all sizes. Page gutter on all four sides (fluid: 40 px large, 20 px small), so the last box on a scrolling Today page ends one gutter from the bottom. Card headers line up: the schedule card has 12 px top padding when its header buttons are 44 px (text buttons or compact icons) and 8 px with 56 px icon buttons, so its eyebrow sits at the same height as the notes and Today’s photo eyebrows.
  • Touch: every control is 44 px or more. Background and Ambient screen tabs use the large PillTabs (56 px) and wrap onto more rows when they don't fit. Sheet close and upload retry/remove are 56 px icon buttons.
Navigation
  • Default: left sidebar (Rail handle), starting Hidden with only the grip on the left edge. The bottom bar below applies when nav=bottom; see Placement option.
  • Bottom bar: Today, Calendar, Weather, Notes, Photos, then Ambient (moon), a 1 px hairline divider, and Settings. The divider and a 24 px gap keep Ambient apart from Settings. Ambient never shows active; it opens Ambient right away from any view, outside the schedule. 56 px icon buttons (IconButton size “touch”), labels via aria-label.
  • Labels: large shows a 14 px label under each icon (Today, Calendar, Weather, Notes, Photos, Ambient, Settings; active label 540 weight, others 400). Tapping the label works like the icon. Bar height 88 px with labels, 72 px without. Small is icons only.
  • Active view = black button. Settings stays active on its sub-pages (Location, Background, Ambient screen).
  • Third-level pages: the back link to the second level (“‹ Settings”, ghost button) sits at the top left of the page, above and outside the white panel, aligned with the panel’s left edge.
  • Bar is hidden on Sign in and Ambient.
  • Hides when idle: after 20 s with no touch the bar slides down and fades (280 ms) and the view takes the full height. Today shows more event rows when they fit. It doesn't hide while a sheet is open, a field is focused, the keyboard is up, or on full-screen Photos (which has its own controls).
  • Handle: while hidden, a white 72×44 pill with a chevron-up sits centered 12 px above the bottom edge (hairline + level-2 shadow, label “Show menu”).
  • Placement option (exploration, prop navPlacement or ?nav=bottom|left|right; default left): the bottom bar is replaced by a side drawer so content gets the full height. Closed state has four handle options (prop handleStyle or ?handle=; default rail). Rail: an 80 px white icon rail on the drawer side (hairline inner edge), the five views as 56 px icon buttons (current view black), Ambient and Settings at the bottom, then a 56 px X (“Hide sidebar”) as the last item, 8 px below Settings; tapping it hides the rail (Hidden). The page is inset by 80 px. An expand handle straddles the rail’s inner edge, vertically centered: a 5×72 black pill, centered on the edge inside a 56×96 touch area, labelled “Expand menu”. It fades out while the drawer is open or in full screen. While the drawer is expanded the rail itself fades out (180 ms), so only the drawer panel shows on that edge. Three modes: Rail → tap the handle → Expanded (the drawer opens; its header has the product name “Glance” (Geist 540, 24 px); a “Close” row (X icon + label, same 64 px pill row style as the items, aria-label “Close sidebar”) sits last at the bottom below Settings) → Hidden (the rail slides off the edge in 280 ms and the page takes the full width; only the grip stays on that edge, labelled “Show sidebar”; grip: a white 36 × 112 px tab flush with the screen edge (24 × 96 on small), 24 px radius on its inner side, level 1 + 2 shadow, 22 px chevron pointing inward (stroke 2), in a 44 × 144 touch area, 0.97 press) → tapping the grip returns to Rail. Picking a view from the expanded drawer returns to Rail. On load Today starts Hidden (?rail=1 starts on Rail). Opening any view other than Today always brings back the Rail, even if it was hidden; it hides with the rail’s X (“Hide sidebar”), the drawer’s Close, or after 15 s idle. Going to Today leaves the sidebar as it is. No hint text is shown. Corner: a white 56 px pill (menu icon + current view name, level-2 shadow) in the bottom corner on the drawer side; the offline pill moves up above it. Grip: a 5×72 black pill centered on the edge with a 44×144 invisible touch area. Tab: a white 48×112 tab with a chevron centered on the edge (24 px inner radius, level-2 shadow). All are labelled “Open menu”. Tap opens a 320 px white panel (12 px inset, 24 px radius, hairline, no shadow, no scrim) on the same layer as the page. It slides in over 280 ms while the page is pushed aside by the same amount (332 px) and reflows; tapping outside the drawer or the rail does nothing to them (the tap goes to the page); they close only with their X. With the Rail handle the drawer’s X closes it completely to Hidden; with the other handles it returns to the closed handle. Panel: “Menu” eyebrow, then Today, Calendar, Weather, Notes, Photos as 64 px pill rows (icon + label; current view is the black primary fill), and Ambient and Settings pinned to the bottom under a hairline, and a “Close” row (X icon + label, same row style) always last at the bottom. The X is a fixed footer in both the drawer and the rail: on short windows the items above it scroll and the X stays visible. Picking an item or the close button closes it; changing view also closes it. The tab hides in photo full screen and while the drawer is open. On Ambient and Sign in the sidebar is hidden completely (rail, grip, handles and drawer) and the page takes the full width. Hide-when-idle applies here too: after 15 s without a touch the drawer closes and the rail hides (see Settings › Hide sidebar when idle).
  • Small (≤ 768): the open drawer slides over the page instead of pushing it, with a 60% scrim behind it; tapping the scrim closes the drawer. Small has no Rail state: the page always takes the full width with only the 24 × 96 px grip on the edge (labelled “Open menu”), tapping the grip opens the full drawer directly, and closing the drawer (X, scrim, or picking a view) returns to the grip. Opening a view other than Today does not bring back a rail on small screens. Everything is smaller on small screens: the drawer is 248 px wide with 48 px rows, 16 px labels, 20 px icons and a 20 px “Glance” header. States: Small-screen drawer. Preview open with ?drawer=1.
  • Reveal: any touch shows the bar and still does its normal job; the first tap is never swallowed. The revealed bar floats over the bottom edge (upward level-2 shadow) so nothing moves under the finger. It docks again on the next view change, and hides again after 20 s without a touch.
  • The Back to Today timeout lands on Today with the bar hidden.
  • Settings › Hide sidebar when idle (main panel, under Temperature): a switch (default on). It controls both navigation styles: off keeps the sidebar (15 s) or the bottom bar (20 s) on screen. The subtitle names the time for the current style.
  • Mock: the 20 s timer runs only when Glance opens with no URL parameters, or with bar=auto. URL: bar=hidden|float, barauto=off.
  • Changing view closes any open sheet and resets scroll to top.
Feedback & motion
  • Touch-first: no hover states. Press scales to 0.97 (120 ms); fills never darken.
  • Tab/tile state 180 ms, screen change 280 ms fade, season and background cross-fade 600 ms.
  • Sheets (notes, photos) open over a 60% black scrim covering the bar. Event pages are full screen and slide in from the right (see Calendar › Event page). Closing an unchanged sheet (scrim, Cancel or ×) closes it at once.
  • Discard changes? If the draft differs from what the sheet opened with, Cancel, × or a scrim tap replaces the footer in place with “Discard changes?” and a line naming what’s lost (“This event won’t be added.” · “Your edits to this event won’t be saved.” · “This note won’t be posted.” · “Your edits to this photo won’t be saved.”), plus “Keep editing” (secondary) and “Discard” (red danger button). Scrim taps are ignored while it shows; × confirms the discard. Any edit to a field returns to the normal footer. Closes any open time picker. The idle timeout still discards without asking. URL: discard=1. State Notes C.
Time & data
  • Mock: date and time are fixed to Fri Oct 9, 2026, 10:41 AM. Production: clock ticks every second; event status recomputes every minute.
  • Season “Auto” maps month → season (Mar–May Spring, Jun–Aug Summer, Sep–Nov Fall, Dec–Feb Winter) and re-checks at midnight.
  • Mock: nothing persists on reload. Production: settings, notes, events, photos and background save to the one family account (one email) and sync to every screen signed in with it.
  • Frames are opened via URL: view=, bg=photo, sheet=event|newevent|allday|note|newnote|photo|photometa, discard=1, events=many, events=none, day=, who=, allday=1, time=…, clock=24, season=, empty=1, net=loading|offline|wxerror, kb=1, signin=…, upload=…, wx=…, sub=location|ambient, amb=…, pick=…, geo=… (see each section).

Sign in and account

States →

Account model: one family = one account = one email address. No members or per-person logins. Events, notes, photos and settings belong to the account; every screen signed in with that email shows the same data. Sign up and sign in are one flow: an unknown email creates the account when its code is entered.

1 · Email form
  • Same form for new and returning families. Eyebrow “One account per family”, title “Sign in or get started”, body “Enter your email. We’ll send a 6-digit code to sign this screen in. New here? The same code sets up your family’s Glance.”
  • Hero (season block 1): wordmark; top right shows the date and time (“Friday, October 9 · 10:41 AM”, follows the clock format setting; on small screens the date is short, “Fri, Oct 9”, and wraps under the wordmark if needed) and the season icon (no season text); headline “Your family’s day, at a glance.”; body “Calendar, weather, notes and photos for your family. Sign in to manage them.” Below it, a static, non-interactive preview of a signed-in screen: a white tile “Today · 3 events” (School drop-off 8:00 past, Piano lesson 10:30 now, Soccer practice 4:30 PM), a block-3 weather tile (64°, cloud-sun, “Rain moving in after 4 PM”) and a sticky note (“Soccer cleats are by the door”, Mom, 8:12 AM). The note always sits with the weather tile: stacked under it beside the events tile when the hero is wide enough, side by side below the events tile when it is not. The preview is hidden on small screens. The hero no longer shows a clock.
  • Email must match name@domain.tld, else “Enter a valid email address” (clears on the next keystroke). The lock footer (“Only your family can see its calendar, notes and photos.”) stays in every form state.
  • Terms link: below the lock footer, “By continuing, you agree to the Terms of Service.” (15/330); “Terms of Service” is an underlined 540-weight link (44px hit target) that opens Glance Terms of Service in a new tab.
  • Signed-out variant: when the server stops accepting this screen’s sign-in, the same form shows with a banner, the title “Sign in again” and the email prefilled. See 9. States S–U and K.
2 · Sending
  • Trigger: “Send code” with a valid email. Button reads “Sending…” and is disabled; the email can’t be edited; a soft tile reads “Sending a code to {email}” with a slow-pulsing mail icon.
  • Clears when the server accepts the request → Enter the code. Mock: 1.4 s.
  • Request fails (online): back to the form with the email kept and editable; field error “Couldn’t send. Try again.” in the TextInput error style. The error clears on the next keystroke; Send code retries. Mock: any email containing “fail”. URL: signin=sendfail. State Y.
3 · Enter the code
  • No links: sign-in always happens by typing the code on the screen that asked for it. Identical for new and known emails, so the flow never reveals whether an account exists. Eyebrow “Sign in”, title “Enter the code”, body “We sent a 6-digit code to {email}. It works once, for 10 minutes.”
  • Code field: label “Code”, six white 8 px-radius cells (hairline ring, mono tabular digits) over one numeric input (inputmode=numeric, autocomplete=one-time-code, max 6). Non-digits and spaces are dropped, so pasting “482 913” works. The next empty cell gets the 2 px black focus ring while focused. Focus lands in the field when the step opens.
  • Primary “Sign in” is disabled until 6 digits are in. The 6th digit submits on its own; Sign in does the same. While checking: button reads “Checking…”, field locked. Mock: 0.9 s; 000000 is wrong, any other code is right.
  • Wrong code: field clears, all cells turn red, caption “That code isn’t right. Try again.” in the TextInput error style; clears on the next digit. 5 wrong tries kill the code: “Too many tries. Send a new code.”, field disabled, Send a new code and Use a different email show at once.
  • Below a hairline, ghost “Send a new code” and “Use a different email”. Both are hidden for 10 s after a code is sent; in their place a mono caption counts down “New code or different email in 0:10” … “0:01”. Each new send restarts the count. URL: signin=sent&backin=N sets the start (add hold=1 to freeze it). States D (counting) and L (done).
  • Send a new code → Sending → Enter the code. Only the newest code works; earlier codes stop working.
  • Right code: new email → What’s your name?; known email → Signed in.
  • Email: subject “Your Glance sign-in code”, preheader “{code} is your Glance code. It works once, for 10 minutes.” The code sits large in a white tile on the hero; no button or link. A note says Glance never asks for the code elsewhere. Same email for new and known addresses; hero uses the account’s season block-1 (Auto = send date). See Glance Emails, source emails/sign-in-code.html.
  • “Use a different email” → form, email kept. URL: code=482 prefills digits; signin=checking|wrong|locked. States N, O, P.
4 · First sign-in · What’s your name?
  • Shown only when a new email enters its code, on this screen, before Today. Returning sign-ins skip it.
  • Eyebrow “New family account”, title “What’s your name?”, body “This is the first sign-in for {email}. Your name shows in Settings.” One field “Your name” (placeholder “First name is fine”), primary “Continue”.
  • Required: empty → “Add your name” (clears on the next keystroke). Name is trimmed. “Continue” → Where is this screen? (new accounts only).
  • With the keyboard open, the page pads by the keyboard height and scrolls the field to 24 px below the top of the scroll area (same rule as other full-page forms).
4b · First sign-in · Where is this screen?
  • After the name step, new accounts only, on this screen. Same hero, full-page form and keyboard rule as the name step.
  • Eyebrow “New family account”, title “Where is this screen?”, body “The weather and the clock’s time zone follow this place. You can change it later in Settings.”
  • City or ZIP field, primary “Continue”, secondary “Use my location”; below a hairline, ghost “Skip for now”. Searching, several matches, locating and every failure behave as in Location and time zone.
  • A found place (single match, picked match, or located) saves → Signed in. No time zone confirm here: the first place always sets the zone.
  • Skip for now → Signed in. Cancels any search or locating. Weather then shows the usual setup card; Settings › Location reads “Not set”.
  • Saving fails → stays on the step, the place kept in the field, field error “Couldn’t save. Try again.” Continue retries.
  • URL: signin=location with wx=locating|choose|denied|savefail, kb=1.
5 · Expired code
  • After 10 minutes on Enter the code → “This code has expired” · “Codes work for 10 minutes. Send a new one to {email}.” Actions: “Send a new code”, “Use a different email”. State H.
  • “Send a new code” → Sending → Enter the code. The old code stays dead; only the newest code works.
6 · Offline
  • Trigger: no connection on the form. Soft banner “You’re offline” · “A code can’t be sent right now. Sending turns back on when the screen reconnects.” The send button is disabled; the email can still be typed.
  • Clears on reconnect: banner leaves, button turns back on. Nothing is queued; the person taps Send again.
  • Offline on Enter the code: the code can still be typed; soft banner “You’re offline” · “The code can’t be checked right now. Sign in turns back on when the screen reconnects.” Sign in and Send a new code are disabled; nothing auto-submits until reconnect.
7 · Signed in
  • After the right code (or the last first-sign-in step): check icon, eyebrow “Signed in”, title “Welcome, {first name}” for a new account or “Welcome back, {first name}” for a returning one, body “Opening Today…”.
  • Timer: Today opens after 2.2 s. No action needed.

9 · Signed out by the server
  • Trigger: the screen is online and the server no longer accepts its sign-in (for example, the family’s account was reset). Rare: sign-ins don’t expire in v1, and Sign out on another screen signs out only that screen. Never while offline; an offline screen keeps working from saved data and checks again on reconnect. No reason is shown.
  • Shows the normal email form (same hero, lock footer, no nav bar) with a soft banner on top: “This screen was signed out” · “Sign in again to keep the calendar, notes and photos up to date.” Title “Sign in again”, body “Check your family’s email below. We’ll send a code that signs this screen back in.”
  • Email is prefilled with the account’s last email and stays editable. Send code → Sending → Enter the code → Signed in work as usual.
  • Waiting changes (events or notes saved but not synced, shown as “Waiting to post”): the banner adds “Changes waiting on this screen: {n}. They’ll be sent after you sign in.” Same wording for 1 and for n. They stay on this screen until sign-in finishes.
  • Different email: inline, no confirm step. When the field holds a different address and changes are waiting, a soft tile under the field reads “Changes waiting on this screen belong to {previous email}. They’ll be removed from this screen if a different email signs in.” with ghost “Use {previous email}” (restores the email). The banner drops “They’ll be sent…”. Sending stays allowed; nothing is removed until the other email actually signs in, so going back is always possible before that.
  • Timing: never interrupts an edit. If an event or note sheet is open or a field is focused, the current view stays as it is. Save or Post keeps the change on this screen as “Waiting to post” and adds it to the count; Cancel discards as usual. The form shows as soon as the sheet closes, or at the normal idle timeout (which pauses while a field is focused, and discards open sheets as it does today).

Location and time zone

One place per account sets the weather and the time zone. The clock, Today, the calendar, event times, hourly forecast and ambient screen all follow the place’s time zone, even when the device’s own clock is set to another one (for example a wall screen left on UTC). US places only. The same setup card appears in three spots: Weather setup, Settings › Location and first sign-in.

Setup card

  • Use my location shows only when the device can locate itself: the browser offers location and its permission isn’t already blocked. Otherwise the card is exactly as before.
  • Typed search: Searching, Unknown place and Several matches as in Weather. A single match saves at once.
Locating · timing rule
  • Tap → the button row is replaced at once by “Finding where this screen is…” · “If the browser asks, choose Allow.” with a slow-pulsing locate icon.
  • The browser’s permission question is not timed: we wait while it’s open. Once allowed, the position has 10 s (low accuracy; a reading up to 10 min old is fine). The place lookup then has 5 s. Past either limit → the matching failure.
  • The field stays editable. Typing cancels locating (the late result is dropped) and brings the buttons back. Leaving the page or Skip for now also cancels.
  • Found → saved at once, same as a single search match: the forecast loads on Weather; Settings returns to Settings (or shows the time zone confirm); sign-in goes to Signed in. Mock: 2.6 s, always Portland, OR; geo=denied|unavailable|outside|lookup|none makes the tap fail or hides the button.
Failures
  • Each returns to the card with the draft kept, as a soft row under the field (not a red field error, since the field wasn’t at fault). It clears on the next keystroke.
  • Permission denied (map-pin-off): “Location is off for this screen. Type a city or ZIP instead.” Use my location hides from then on, since the browser won’t ask again.
  • Position unavailable or timed out (locate-off): “Couldn’t find where this screen is. Try again, or type a city or ZIP.”
  • Outside the US (globe): “This screen seems to be outside the US. Glance works for US places only. Type a US city or ZIP.”
  • Lookup failed (search-x): “Couldn’t look up this place. Try again, or type a city or ZIP.”
  • “Try again” = tap Use my location again. Only the card changes; the rest of the screen and the saved location stay as they were.

  • Decided: confirm, but only when the new place is in a different time zone. Reasons: it moves the clock and every event on every screen of the account, and the likeliest cause is a wrong pick from “2 places called Portland” (Oregon vs Maine = 3 hours).

Naming the time zone
  • Always “{Name} time”, the way families say it: Eastern time, Central time, Mountain time, Arizona time (most of Arizona, no clock change), Pacific time, Alaska time, Hawaii time. Territories: Atlantic time (Puerto Rico, US Virgin Islands), Chamorro time (Guam, Northern Mariana Islands), Samoa time.
  • No abbreviations (PT, PDT), no offsets, no “standard” or “daylight”, no technical zone IDs. The name doesn’t change twice a year.
  • Where it shows: the Settings row summary, the Location page, the confirm, and match rows (“Oregon, United States · Pacific time”). Nowhere else; the clock itself carries no zone label.
  • Until a place is set, times follow the device’s clock.

Event notifications

Design →

Reminders for upcoming events: on the display over any view, quietly on Ambient during quiet hours, and as a push notification on phones and tablets when Glance is closed. Not yet in the clickable mockup.

When
  • Each event fires 15 min before, at start, or both, per its Reminder field. Off = never. All-day events have no reminder.
  • Every signed-in screen shows it at the same moment. Phones and tablets with Glance open show the on-display strip; closed, they get the push.
  • Missed: a screen that was offline or asleep shows a reminder once if the event hasn’t started yet, with the real time left (“In 6 min”). After the start it’s skipped.
  • Moving, editing the time of, or deleting an event cancels its pending reminders and schedules new ones.
On-display strip

Data needed

Content
  • Events: id, start timestamp, end timestamp (optional: “No end time”), all-day flag, title, where (place), who (member ids, may be empty), notes, reminder (off | at start | 15 min | both; planned, see Event notifications). No color: it comes from who.
  • Members: id, name, color (one of 12). Created from an event’s Who field only.
  • Weather: current (temp, condition, high, low, feels like, wind, humidity, UV, sunset), 8 hourly points, 7 days (high, low, condition, precip %).
  • Notes: id, text, author, created time, rotation (no color; notes always use the season paper tone).
  • Photos: id, title, date, place, image.
Account and place
  • Location (one per account): place name, state, coordinates and its time zone. Sets the weather and every displayed time. Unset = device clock.
  • Account: one family = one account = one email. Stores the person’s name and email. Screen label is “Glance”.
Settings (persisted)
  • Season (Auto or a fixed season), clock 12/24 h, °F/°C, background {mode, photoId, dim, rotate}, idle timeout, note expiry, default reminder and chime (planned). Push on/off is per device, not per account.
  • Ambient schedule {mode: same|split|off, all: {start, end}, wknd: {start, end}} (minutes of the day; wknd = Fri & Sat nights when split; end earlier than start = next morning; start ≠ end). Default every night 6:00 PM – 7:30 AM; weekend default 9:00 PM – 9:00 AM.
