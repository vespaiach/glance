Glance · behavior notes

What the frames can’t show: interactions, every state per screen, time-driven and background behavior, and where the mock differs from production. Each section maps to one board. Mock marks behavior that is faked in the prototype and needs a real implementation.

Global

Layout
  • Side navigation hides itself after 15 s with no touch or key press: an open drawer closes and the rail slides away, leaving the “Show sidebar” grip. It stays while a sheet, the keyboard or a text field is active. Tapping the grip brings it back and restarts the 15 s.
  • Fluid web app, not a scaled 1920 canvas. Breakpoints by window width: wide ≥ 1200, mid 1000–1199, narrow 600–999, phone < 600. Layout re-evaluates live on resize.
  • Wide and mid fit one screen; narrow stacks into a single scrolling column.
  • Today uses the Now panel layout (3a) at every width and fits the viewport with no scroll above 1180 px. Three columns above 1180 px (landscape): Now panel (clock, weather now, current or next event) in the left column at full height, schedule in the middle, notes over Picture of the day in the right column (two note columns from 1600 px). On three-column screens under 880 px tall Today shows one note so nothing is cut off; “All notes” opens the rest. Two columns from 1180 px down to 1024 px (iPad landscape): Now panel and schedule fill the screen side by side (top row at least 660 px, or the window height minus 160 px, so the Now panel fits clock, weather, the 6-hour tile and the event); notes and Picture of the day sit in a second row below, reached by scrolling. Portrait wider than 1024 px: Now panel beside notes and Picture of the day, schedule at full width below. Below 1024 px (iPad portrait and phone): one scrolling column, Now panel, schedule, notes, Picture of the day. Notes use one column below 600 px. Picture of the day has its own white panel: eyebrow, “All photos”, the photo filling the panel (tap opens it in Photos) and its title; with no photos it shows “Upload a photo”. The schedule header shows “Add event” and “Full calendar” as text buttons from 1440 px; below that they are icon buttons (plus, calendar) so they stay on the header row. The schedule fills its card’s height. The clock and temperature scale with the panel’s width. Body type scales up past 1920×1080, up to 1.5×. Earlier options can still be previewed with ?lay=a|b|c|s.
  • Phone (390×844): one column everywhere. Today matches iPad portrait: Now panel, schedule, notes, Picture of the day. Hourly and 7-day forecast scroll sideways. Calendar: the date control replaces the week strip (see Calendar › Date control); the timeline has no weather lane or rain chips, so plans use the full width; the day timeline is 720 px tall and the page scrolls. Photo and background thumbs go to 2 columns. Nav: all seven buttons (44 px) spread edge to edge across the bar with no brand slot, so Settings stays on screen at 390 px. The offline pill sits above the bar.
  • 1280×800 Android tablets use the laptop layout (Today in three columns); iPad portrait (820) uses narrow. 1920×1080 uses wide.
  • Photo background: every zone with text sits on a white panel (radius 24), so every view reads on a photo.
  • Canvas: the season-color background (soft gray-tinted) sits behind white panels, the reverse of the design system's white gutters. Deliberate, so one layout works on both the season color and a photo.
  • Type: smaller than the design system's 1920 kiosk scale so the app fits laptops and tablets. Clock 84–128 px (6.8 vw), temperature 76–96 px. Mono captions are 14 px minimum (16 px on Ambient). All text is black; no gray or dimmed text.
  • Spacing follows the 8 px scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32).
  • Touch: every control is 44 px or more. Settings, Background and Ambient tabs use the large PillTabs (56 px) and wrap onto more rows when they don't fit. Sheet close and upload retry/remove are 56 px icon buttons.
Navigation
  • Default: left sidebar (Rail handle), starting Hidden with only the grip on the left edge. The bottom bar below applies when nav=bottom; see Placement option.
  • Bottom bar: Today, Calendar, Weather, Notes, Photos, then Ambient (moon), a 1 px hairline divider, and Settings. The divider and a 24 px gap keep Ambient apart from Settings. Ambient never shows active; it opens Ambient right away from any view, outside the schedule. 56 px icon buttons (IconButton size “touch”), labels via aria-label.
  • Labels: wide and mid show a 14 px label under each icon (Today, Calendar, Weather, Notes, Photos, Ambient, Settings; active label 540 weight, others 400). Tapping the label works like the icon. Bar height 88 px with labels, 72 px without. Narrow and phone are icons only.
  • Active view = black button. Settings stays active on its Background sub-page.
  • Bar is hidden on Sign in and Ambient.
  • Hides when idle: after 20 s with no touch the bar slides down and fades (280 ms) and the view takes the full height. Today shows more event rows when they fit. It doesn't hide while a sheet is open, a field is focused, the keyboard is up, or on full-screen Photos (which has its own controls).
  • Handle: while hidden, a white 72×44 pill with a chevron-up sits centered 12 px above the bottom edge (hairline + level-2 shadow, label “Show menu”).
  • Placement option (exploration, prop navPlacement or ?nav=bottom|left|right; default left): the bottom bar is replaced by a side drawer so content gets the full height. Closed state has four handle options (prop handleStyle or ?handle=; default rail). Rail: an 80 px white icon rail on the drawer side (hairline inner edge), the five views as 56 px icon buttons (current view black), Ambient and Settings at the bottom, then a 56 px X (“Hide sidebar”) as the last item, 8 px below Settings; tapping it hides the rail (Hidden). The page is inset by 80 px. An expand handle straddles the rail’s inner edge, vertically centered: the same 5×72 black grip pill used in Hidden, centered on the edge inside a 56×96 touch area, labelled “Expand menu”. It fades out while the drawer is open or in full screen. While the drawer is expanded the rail itself fades out (180 ms), so only the drawer panel shows on that edge. Three modes: Rail → tap the handle → Expanded (the drawer opens; its header has the product name “Glance” (Geist 540, 24 px); a “Close” row (X icon + label, same 64 px pill row style as the items, aria-label “Close sidebar”) sits last at the bottom below Settings) → Hidden (the rail slides off the edge in 280 ms and the page takes the full width; only the grip stays on that edge, labelled “Show sidebar”) → tapping the grip returns to Rail. Picking a view from the expanded drawer returns to Rail. On load Today starts Hidden (?rail=1 starts on Rail). Opening any view other than Today always brings back the Rail, even if it was hidden; the only way to hide it is Expanded → X. Going to Today leaves the sidebar as it is. No hint text is shown. Corner: a white 56 px pill (menu icon + current view name, level-2 shadow) in the bottom corner on the drawer side; the offline pill moves up above it. Grip: a 5×72 black pill centered on the edge with a 44×144 invisible touch area. Tab: a white 48×112 tab with a chevron centered on the edge (24 px inner radius, level-2 shadow). All are labelled “Open menu”. Tap opens a 320 px white panel (12 px inset, 24 px radius, hairline, no shadow, no scrim) on the same layer as the page. It slides in over 280 ms while the page is pushed aside by the same amount (332 px) and reflows; tapping outside the drawer or the rail does nothing to them (the tap goes to the page); they close only with their X. With the Rail handle the drawer’s X closes it completely to Hidden; with the other handles it returns to the closed handle. Panel: “Menu” eyebrow, then Today, Calendar, Weather, Notes, Photos as 64 px pill rows (icon + label; current view is the black primary fill), and Ambient and Settings pinned to the bottom under a hairline, and a “Close” row (X icon + label, same row style) always last at the bottom. The X is a fixed footer in both the drawer and the rail: on short windows the items above it scroll and the X stays visible. Picking an item or the close button closes it; changing view also closes it. The tab hides in photo full screen and while the drawer is open. On Ambient and Sign in the sidebar is hidden completely (rail, grip, handles and drawer) and the page takes the full width. Hide-when-idle does not apply in drawer mode.
  • Phone (< 600): the open drawer slides over the page instead of pushing it, with a 60% scrim behind it; tapping the scrim closes the drawer. Everything is smaller on phone: the rail is 56 px wide (page inset 56 px) with 44 px icon buttons and a 4×56 px grip; the drawer is 248 px wide with 48 px rows, 16 px labels, 20 px icons and a 20 px “Glance” header. Frames: Phone drawer. Preview open with ?drawer=1.
  • Reveal: any touch shows the bar and still does its normal job; the first tap is never swallowed. The revealed bar floats over the bottom edge (upward level-2 shadow) so nothing moves under the finger. It docks again on the next view change, and hides again after 20 s without a touch.
  • The Back to Today timeout lands on Today with the bar hidden.
  • Settings › Hide bar when idle (main panel, under Temperature): On · Off (default On). Off keeps the bar docked. Has no effect with the sidebar.
  • Mock: the 20 s timer runs only when Glance opens with no URL parameters, or with bar=auto. URL: bar=hidden|float, barauto=off.
  • Changing view closes any open sheet and resets scroll to top.
Feedback & motion
  • Touch-first: no hover states. Press scales to 0.97 (120 ms); fills never darken.
  • Tab/tile state 180 ms, screen change 280 ms fade, season and background cross-fade 600 ms.
  • Sheets open over a 60% black scrim covering the bar. Closing an unchanged sheet (scrim, Cancel or ×) closes it at once.
  • Discard changes? If the draft differs from what the sheet opened with, Cancel, × or a scrim tap replaces the footer in place with “Discard changes?” and a line naming what’s lost (“This event won’t be added.” · “Your edits to this event won’t be saved.” · “This note won’t be posted.” · “Your edits to this photo won’t be saved.”), plus “Keep editing” (secondary) and “Discard” (red danger button). Scrim taps are ignored while it shows; × confirms the discard. Any edit to a field returns to the normal footer. Closes any open time picker. The idle timeout still discards without asking. URL: discard=1. Board frame Notes C.
Time & data
  • Mock: date and time are fixed to Fri Oct 9, 2026, 10:41 AM. Production: clock ticks every second; event status recomputes every minute.
  • Season “Auto” maps month → season (Mar–May Spring, Jun–Aug Summer, Sep–Nov Fall, Dec–Feb Winter) and re-checks at midnight.
  • Mock: nothing persists on reload. Production: settings, notes, events, photos and background save to the one family account (one email) and sync to every screen signed in with it.
  • Frames are opened via URL: view=, bg=photo, sheet=event|newevent|allday|note|newnote|photo|photometa, discard=1, events=many, day=, who=, allday=1, day=, time=…, clock=24, season=, empty=1, net=loading|offline|wxerror, kb=1, signin=…, upload=…, wx=…, sub=location|ambient, amb=…, pick=…, geo=… (see each section).

Sign in and account

Open board →

Account model: one family = one account = one email address. No members or per-person logins. Events, notes, photos and settings belong to the account; every screen signed in with that email shows the same data. Sign up and sign in are one flow: an unknown email creates the account when its link is opened.

1 · Email form
  • Same form for new and returning families. Eyebrow “One account per family”, title “Sign in or get started”, body “Enter your email. We’ll send a link that signs this screen in. New here? The same link sets up your family’s Glance.”
  • Hero: “Calendar, weather, notes and photos for your family. Sign in to manage them.”
  • Email must match name@domain.tld, else “Enter a valid email address” (clears on the next keystroke). The lock footer (“Only your family can see its calendar, notes and photos.”) stays in every form state.
  • Signed-out variant: when the server stops accepting this screen’s sign-in, the same form shows with a banner, the title “Sign in again” and the email prefilled. See 9. Board frames S–W and K.
2 · Sending
  • Trigger: “Send sign-in link” with a valid email. Button reads “Sending…” and is disabled; the email can’t be edited; a soft tile reads “Sending a link to {email}” with a slow-pulsing mail icon.
  • Clears when the server accepts the request → Check your email. Mock: 1.4 s.
  • Request fails (online): back to the form with the email kept and editable; field error “Couldn’t send. Try again.” in the TextInput error style. The error clears on the next keystroke; Send sign-in link retries. Mock: any email containing “fail”. URL: signin=sendfail. Board frame Y.
3 · Check your email
  • Identical for new and known emails, so the flow never reveals whether an account exists: “We sent a link to {email}. Open it and this screen signs in by itself.” + “Link expires in 10 minutes”.
  • This screen is now the waiting screen. It polls (or gets a push) until the link is opened, then moves to Finish on your phone, Signed in, or Expired.
  • “Use a different email” → form, email kept. Mock: “I opened the link” = link opened on this device (maria@example.com is the known account; any other email is new).
4 · First sign-in · What’s your name?
  • Shown only when a new email opens its link, on the device that opened it, before Today. Returning sign-ins skip it.
  • Eyebrow “New family account”, title “What’s your name?”, body “This is the first sign-in for {email}. Your name shows in Settings.” One field “Your name” (placeholder “First name is fine”), primary “Continue”.
  • Required: empty → “Add your name” (clears on the next keystroke). Name is trimmed. “Continue” → Where is this screen? (new accounts only).
  • With the keyboard open, the page pads by the keyboard height and scrolls the field to 24 px below the top of the scroll area (same rule as other full-page forms).
  • If the link opened on a phone, the name and location steps run on the phone (its location is usually home; it can still type a city). The waiting screen shows “Finish on your phone” · “The link opened on another device. Add your name and this screen’s location there, and this screen signs in by itself.” with a “Waiting for your phone” tile. It signs in by itself when the name is saved. Mock: “I finished on my phone”.
4b · First sign-in · Where is this screen?
  • After the name step, new accounts only, on the device that opened the link. Same hero, full-page form and keyboard rule as the name step.
  • Eyebrow “New family account”, title “Where is this screen?”, body “The weather and the clock’s time zone follow this place. You can change it later in Settings.”
  • City or ZIP field, primary “Continue”, secondary “Use my location”; below a hairline, ghost “Skip for now”. Searching, several matches, locating and every failure behave as in Location and time zone.
  • A found place (single match, picked match, or located) saves → Signed in. No time zone confirm here: the first place always sets the zone.
  • Skip for now → Signed in. Cancels any search or locating. Weather then shows the usual setup card; Settings › Location reads “Not set”.
  • Saving fails → stays on the step, the place kept in the field, field error “Couldn’t save. Try again.” Continue retries.
  • URL: signin=location with wx=locating|choose|denied|savefail, kb=1.
5 · Expired or used link
  • Waiting screen: after 10 minutes with no open → “This link has expired” · “Sign-in links work for 10 minutes. Send a new one to {email}.” Actions: “Send a new link”, “Use a different email”.
  • Device that opened the link: link older than 10 minutes or already used → “This link doesn’t work anymore” · “It expired or was already used. Each link works once, for 10 minutes. Send a new one to {email}.” Action: “Send a new link”.
  • “Send a new link” → Sending → Check your email (on that device). The old link stays dead; only the newest link works.
6 · Offline
  • Trigger: no connection on the form. Soft banner “You’re offline” · “A link can’t be sent right now. Sending turns back on when the screen reconnects.” The send button is disabled; the email can still be typed.
  • Clears on reconnect: banner leaves, button turns back on. Nothing is queued; the person taps Send again.
  • Offline while waiting: keep Check your email; resume polling on reconnect.
7 · Signed in
  • On the waiting screen (or the device that opened the link) after sign-in succeeds: check icon, eyebrow “Signed in”, title “Welcome, {first name}” for a new account or “Welcome back, {first name}” for a returning one, body “Opening Today…”.
  • Timer: Today opens after 2.2 s. No action needed.
8 · Settings › Account
  • Header: “Signed in as {name} · {email}”. Card: initial avatar, name, email. No family name anywhere.
  • Signed-in screens: eyebrow “{n} screens signed in”, then a soft-tile list of every device signed in with this email. Row: device-type icon (monitor, tablet, smartphone) in a white circle, device name, mono caption “This screen” for the current device (listed first, with a seasonal accent dot) or “Active {time} ago / yesterday / {date}” for others, newest first. Read-only in v1: no rename or remote sign-out. Mock: list is fixed sample data.
  • Sign in another device: secondary “Sign in another device” (qr-code icon) under the list. Tap → replaced in place by a block-1 tile: QR code on white, title “Scan to sign in”, body “Open the camera on the other device and point it at the code. It signs in with {email}.”, mono caption “Code expires in {m:ss}” counting down from 5:00 each second (tabular figures), ghost “Done” closes it and stops the timer. The code opens a one-time sign-in link for this account, skipping the email step; at 0:00 a new code is issued and the countdown restarts at 5:00 while the tile is open. The new device joins the list once signed in. Mock: the QR is a static pattern.
  • “Sign out” signs out this screen only; other screens with the same email stay signed in. Caption “Sign out this device only.”: info icon + black mono caption on the left, button on the right (space-between), below a hairline. Styled as a note so it doesn’t read as a second action. No confirm step. Returns to the email form, empty.
  • Out of scope: changing name or email, deleting the account, more than one email per account.
9 · Signed out by the server
  • Trigger: the screen is online and the server no longer accepts its sign-in (for example, the family’s account was reset). Rare: sign-ins don’t expire in v1, and Sign out on another screen signs out only that screen. Never while offline; an offline screen keeps working from saved data and checks again on reconnect. No reason is shown.
  • Shows the normal email form (same hero, lock footer, no nav bar) with a soft banner on top: “This screen was signed out” · “Sign in again to keep the calendar, notes and photos up to date.” Title “Sign in again”, body “Check your family’s email below. We’ll send a link that signs this screen back in.”
  • Email is prefilled with the account’s last email and stays editable. Send sign-in link → Sending → Check your email → Signed in work as usual.
  • Waiting changes (events or notes saved but not synced, shown as “Waiting to post”): the banner adds “Changes waiting on this screen: {n}. They’ll be sent after you sign in.” Same wording for 1 and for n. They stay on this screen until sign-in finishes.
  • Different email: inline, no confirm step. When the field holds a different address and changes are waiting, a soft tile under the field reads “Changes waiting on this screen belong to {previous email}. They’ll be removed from this screen if a different email signs in.” with ghost “Use {previous email}” (restores the email). The banner drops “They’ll be sent…”. Sending stays allowed; nothing is removed until the other email actually signs in, so going back is always possible before that.
  • Timing: never interrupts an edit. If an event or note sheet is open or a field is focused, the current view stays as it is. Save or Post keeps the change on this screen as “Waiting to post” and adds it to the count; Cancel discards as usual. The form shows as soon as the sheet closes, or at the normal idle timeout (which pauses while a field is focused, and discards open sheets as it does today).
  • Ambient (quiet hours): Ambient stays, with “Signed out · tap to sign in” in the top row (same style as the offline message). The clock and saved weather keep showing. The next wake tap opens the form instead of Today.
  • Signed in again: the normal “Welcome back, {first name}” confirmation, then Today. Same email: waiting changes sync as usual, no extra screen. Different email: waiting changes are removed from this screen when its sign-in finishes; a new email goes through the name and location steps.
  • Clears only when a sign-in finishes (any email). Reload, Use a different email and an expired link keep the screen signed out and the notice on the form.
  • URL: signin=ended, &pending=3, &switch=1 (different email entered), &sheet=newnote (sheet open when it happens), view=ambient&signin=ended.
URL
  • signin=form|sending|sent|name|location|phone|expired|offline|done|ended. on=link with expired = device that opened the link. acct=returning with done = “Welcome back”. kb=1 adds the keyboard.
  • URL states are snapshots: timers only run after a tap.

Today

Behavior
  • Tap the “Happening now” hero or any plan on the timeline → Event details (Edit inside opens Edit event). Tap a dashed free slot → New event at that time (see Calendar).
  • “Add event” opens New event on today. “Full calendar” → Calendar. “All notes” → Notes.
  • Schedule header buttons: below 1440 px “Add event” and “Full calendar” show icon only, 56 px white IconButtons (plus, calendar-days) with the same labels as aria-label. From 1440 px they are text buttons.
  • Tap a sticky note → Note details (Edit inside opens Edit note). Only the first two notes appear here.
  • Layout: 3a Now panel at every width (see Global › Layout); one scrolling column below 1024 px.
  • Schedule = day timeline (decided: option 1c, elastic hours). The same timeline as Calendar, always for everyone and always today; there is no person filter on Today. It fills the schedule card’s height (min 200 px; 560 px in the one-column layout below 1024 px, where the page scrolls). Hours that have passed shrink more than on Calendar (weight × 0.3) so the rest of the day gets the room. Gutter 80 px and weather lane 112 px on wide; 76/96 on mid and narrow; 52/48 on phone.
States
  • Hero: event running → “Happening now”, title + “{where} · until {end} · {who}”. With several plans running, the hero shows the one that started last (ties: the shorter one); the timeline shows the rest. Nothing running but more later → “Up next”, title + “{where} · {start}”. All ended → “Nothing else today”. Empty day → “Nothing scheduled”.
  • Decided: 3a Now panel (default at every width, phone included; lay=n). 3b Time strip still previews with lay=s. Both merge hero and weather into one zone: clock with date, followed by the current season’s icon (18 px; flower-2 · sun · leaf · snowflake); “{city} · Now”, temperature, “Partly cloudy · High {h}° · Low {l}°”, and “Rain from {time}” (first drizzle or rain hour); then the event with a black pill: “{n} min left” while running, “In {n} min” before the next one (“1 h 20 min” past an hour). No season label and no separate weather card. Tap weather → Weather; tap event → Edit event. No location → “Set weather location” → Weather. Weather error → “Weather unavailable” · “Trying again in 1 min.”
  • Picture of the day panel (3a): its own white panel with eyebrow, “All photos”, the photo filling the panel and its title. Placement follows Global › Layout: under notes (three columns), beside notes in the row below (1024–1180 px), last in the one-column stack. Tap the photo → Photos with that photo featured and the slideshow paused. No photos → “Upload a photo” → Photos. It stays on the photo background.
  • Picture of the day in the hero (earlier option, lay=a preview only) sits at the bottom of the hero, 32 px below the event: square thumbnail sized clamp(56 px, min(14vh, 40% of the slot), 120 px) so it shrinks on short windows and Today still fits with no scroll (radius 8), 20 px gap, then mono “Picture of the day” (14 px) over the title (18 px / 480), max width 420 px. Text always sits beside the thumbnail; the image also caps at 40% of the hero width so the text keeps room; both one line with ellipsis. No panel behind it. Tap → Photos with that same photo in the featured frame (selected ring in the grid) and the slideshow paused, so it stays put until the user swipes or presses play. With no photos the same slot reads “Upload a photo” beside an outlined square of the same size with an image-plus icon; tap → Photos. The schedule column no longer holds a photo card, so it gets the full height for rows.
  • Plans: past = white with a hairline and struck-through title; now = 2 px black ring and NOW pill; upcoming = the person’s color, or the season soft color with person dots for shared plans.
  • Eyebrow: “Today · {n} events” (all-day events included; “1 event” singular) or “Today · No events”.
  • All-day events: soft tiles (56 px min, radius 8) above the timeline, in event order: mono “ALL DAY”, person dot (soft for shared), title, then “{who} · {place}”. Tap → Event details. They never become the hero, never show as Happening now or Up next, and never strike through.
  • Busy days: no “See all” row any more. Every plan from 5 AM to 9 PM stays on the timeline; elastic hours and side-by-side columns make room. URL events=many adds 4 more plans (22).
  • Weather with no location → “Weather needs a location” · “Set the city once. The forecast and the clock follow it.” → Set location opens the Weather setup card (decided: stays on Weather, not Settings › Location; it’s the zone being fixed, and the card has the same search and Use my location).
  • On photo background: the Now panel, schedule, notes and Picture of the day all sit on white panels, so they read on either background. (In the lay=a preview the hero’s picture of the day is dropped.)
Not visible
  • Rows move past → now → upcoming in real time; hero swaps at each start/end.
  • Past / now / upcoming come from start and end against the clock (fixed at 10:41 AM in the mock). Times are stored as minutes of the day. Production needs real timestamps.
  • Picture of the day: one photo per calendar day, the same on every screen of the account; it changes at midnight in the location’s time zone. First choice: a photo taken on today’s date in an earlier year (eyebrow “On this day · {year}”). Otherwise the next photo in a shuffled cycle: every photo shows once before any repeats; photos added mid-cycle join the end of it. Only finished uploads count. If today’s photo is deleted, the next in the cycle replaces it at once. Mock: fixed to Cannon Beach.
  • Multi-day events are not designed.

Calendar and events

Open board →
Behavior
  • Board: the Calendar board shows A (today, everyone) and C (Family sheet) at six sizes, B (date picker) on phone and D (remove confirm) on large display and phone, plus a short behavior summary; other states are reached by tapping or by URL.
  • Layout (decided: option 1c): week strip card on top, then one card with the person filter, the free-time line, “Add event”, all-day tiles and the day timeline. The month grid is gone.
  • Week strip: “{Mon d – d}” eyebrow and month name (wide and mid), chevrons (56 px; 44 px on phone), then 7 day pills: weekday, date, one 6 px dot per person who has a plan that day, and the day’s forecast icon + high on wide and mid. Selected = black; today keeps a 2 px ring. Days with no forecast (past days, more than 6 days out) show no weather. Chevrons step a week; landing on this week selects today, any other week selects its Sunday. 600 px and up (phone uses the date control).
  • Date control (phone only, under 600 px; replaces the week strip): one full-width white card button. Left: year (mono eyebrow) and month name (28 px). Right: the selected date as a black pill (weekday + date) with a chevron-down, so it reads as tappable. Tap opens the date picker.
  • Date picker: centered modal on the scrim (max 480 px wide, up to 760 px tall, radius 24). Header: “Pick a date”, a ghost Today button and Close. Body: a vertical list of days, Aug 2026 to Jan 2027, with sticky month headers; scroll up for past dates, down for upcoming. Each row (56 px pill): weekday, date, one 8 px dot per person with a plan, “Today” tag. Selected = black; today keeps a 2 px ring. Opens scrolled so the selected day is centered. Tap a day → selects it and closes; Today → selects today and closes; scrim or Close → closes with no change. URL: dp=open.
  • Person filter (redesigned for large families): a row of soft pills, 48 px tall (44 on phone), 8 px apart, one per member: 12 px color dot + name, 16 px / 480, soft fill (surface-soft) when not selected so the pills read as a row that scrolls sideways. No Everyone button: all members selected = everyone. Default is everyone: every dot filled, no black. Tap a member while everyone shows → only that person (black pill). Tap more members to add them; tap a selected member to remove them. Selecting every member goes back to everyone. Removing the last one leaves nobody selected: every dot is a hollow ring and the calendar is empty (no plans, no all-day tiles, no free slots); it is not treated as everyone. Location and time zone

Weather board →Settings board →Account board →

One place per account sets the weather and the time zone. The clock, Today, the calendar, event times, hourly forecast and ambient screen all follow the place’s time zone, even when the device’s own clock is set to another one (for example a wall screen left on UTC). US places only. The same setup card appears in three spots: Weather setup, Settings › Location and first sign-in.

Setup card
  • Field “City or ZIP code”, then one row: the primary (“Show weather” on Weather, “Search” in Settings, “Continue” at sign-in) and secondary “Use my location” (locate icon).
  • Use my location shows only when the device can locate itself: the browser offers location and its permission isn’t already blocked. Otherwise the card is exactly as before.
  • Typed search: Searching, Unknown place and Several matches as in Weather. A single match saves at once.
Locating · timing rule
  • Tap → the button row is replaced at once by “Finding where this screen is…” · “If the browser asks, choose Allow.” with a slow-pulsing locate icon.
  • The browser’s permission question is not timed: we wait while it’s open. Once allowed, the position has 10 s (low accuracy; a reading up to 10 min old is fine). The place lookup then has 5 s. Past either limit → the matching failure.
  • The field stays editable. Typing cancels locating (the late result is dropped) and brings the buttons back. Leaving the page or Skip for now also cancels.
  • Found → saved at once, same as a single search match: the forecast loads on Weather; Settings returns to Settings (or shows the time zone confirm); sign-in goes to Signed in. Mock: 2.6 s, always Portland, OR; geo=denied|unavailable|outside|lookup|none makes the tap fail or hides the button.
Failures
  • Each returns to the card with the draft kept, as a soft row under the field (not a red field error, since the field wasn’t at fault). It clears on the next keystroke.
  • Permission denied (map-pin-off): “Location is off for this screen. Type a city or ZIP instead.” Use my location hides from then on, since the browser won’t ask again.
  • Position unavailable or timed out (locate-off): “Couldn’t find where this screen is. Try again, or type a city or ZIP.”
  • Outside the US (globe): “This screen seems to be outside the US. Glance works for US places only. Type a US city or ZIP.”
  • Lookup failed (search-x): “Couldn’t look up this place. Try again, or type a city or ZIP.”
  • “Try again” = tap Use my location again. Only the card changes; the rest of the screen and the saved location stay as they were.
Settings › Location
  • Row: first in the Settings panel. “Location” · “Sets the weather and the time zone” · summary “Portland, OR · Pacific time” or “Not set”.
  • Page (like Background: “Settings” back button, white panel): “Now set to” tile with the place and “Pacific time · 10:41 AM” (or “Not set” · “Times follow this device’s own clock”), then “Change location” with the setup card. Right column: what the location sets (Weather, Time zone) and “The clock and event times follow Pacific time, even if this device is set to a different time.”
  • Same time zone → saves at once and returns to Settings; the row summary updates.
New time zone · confirm
  • Decided: confirm, but only when the new place is in a different time zone. Reasons: it moves the clock and every event on every screen of the account, and the likeliest cause is a wrong pick from “2 places called Portland” (Oregon vs Maine = 3 hours).
  • The card is replaced in place by a highlight block: “Time zone change” · “Switch to Eastern time?” · “Portland, ME is in Eastern time. The clock moves 3 hours ahead, and event times change to match on every screen.” Two tiles: “Now · Pacific time 10:41 AM” and “After · Eastern time 1:41 PM”, plus one example: “Soccer practice at 3:30 PM will show at 6:30 PM.”
  • Primary “Switch to Eastern time” saves and returns to Settings; the clock jumps right away (no animation). Secondary “Keep Pacific time” goes back to the card with the draft kept.
  • Zones that match today but split later in the year (Arizona vs Pacific in October): same confirm, body “The time is the same right now, but it can differ later in the year.”
  • Not asked on first set (Weather setup, sign-in): the first place always sets the zone, even if the clock jumps from the device’s zone.
  • Events are saved as moments in time, so they keep their moment and show at the new local time.
Naming the time zone
  • Always “{Name} time”, the way families say it: Eastern time, Central time, Mountain time, Arizona time (most of Arizona, no clock change), Pacific time, Alaska time, Hawaii time. Territories: Atlantic time (Puerto Rico, US Virgin Islands), Chamorro time (Guam, Northern Mariana Islands), Samoa time.
  • No abbreviations (PT, PDT), no offsets, no “standard” or “daylight”, no technical zone IDs. The name doesn’t change twice a year.
  • Where it shows: the Settings row summary, the Location page, the confirm, and match rows (“Oregon, United States · Pacific time”). Nowhere else; the clock itself carries no zone label.
  • Until a place is set, times follow the device’s clock.

  • Signed out by the server: Ambient stays; “Signed out · tap to sign in” in the top row, same style. Clock and saved weather keep showing.
Not visible
  • Enters automatically at the dim time set in Settings › Ambient screen (Sun – Thu or Fri & Sat set when weekends differ), leaves at the wake time. Never when the schedule is Off. After a wake tap during hours, it returns after the idle timeout.
  • Burn-in protection (slow content drift) and screen dimming are recommended, not designed.

First use · empty states

Open board →
  • Empty states are per zone, not per app: each clears independently once its data exists (first event, first note, first photo, location set).
  • Every empty state has one action. It opens the matching sheet, or goes to the screen that holds the setup (Set location → Weather, Upload → Photos).
  • New accounts are asked for the location once, at sign-in (“Where is this screen?”, skippable). Skipping leaves Weather’s setup card in place.
  • Not designed: a guided setup that chains location → first event → photos. A new screen joins the family by signing in with the account email, or by scanning the QR code from Settings › Account on a signed-in screen.

Loading, offline and keyboard

Open board →
Loading
  • Each source (calendar, weather, notes, photos) loads on its own; zones fill in as they arrive. Clock and season are local and never wait.
  • Skeletons keep the final shape so nothing jumps. They pulse slowly (1.2 s opacity, 0.06–0.1).
  • Skeletons only on first load with no saved data. Later refreshes swap silently.
  • Over 10 s → that zone shows its failure state.
Source failure (online)
  • Only the failing zone changes: icon, “{Source} unavailable”, retry countdown, “Try again”. Weather has this state on Today and on the Weather view.
  • If saved data exists, keep showing it instead and mark the zone “Updated {time}”.
  • Auto-retry at 1, 2, 5 min, then every 5 min.
Offline
  • Black pill, fixed bottom-left above the bar (12 px above it without labels, 24 px with labels, 84 px on phone): “Offline · showing saved data” + last sync time (time hidden on narrow and phone, and on Weather where the banner already shows it). Appears after 30 s offline to avoid flicker; leaves on reconnect.
  • Saved data stays visible. Event statuses keep updating from the local clock.
  • Weather older than 1 hour shows “Forecast from {time}” banner.
  • Edits queue locally: sheet shows an offline banner, save reads “Save for later”, new notes show “Waiting to post” until synced.
  • Ambient: same message in the top row, white 16 px mono text.
  • A screen is never signed out by the server while offline. Queued edits stay queued; if the server rejects the sign-in on reconnect, see Sign in and account, 9.
On-screen keyboard
  • The keyboard is the OS one (drawn as a mock). It opens when a field is focused and covers ~36% of the height (260–380 px).
  • The sheet’s area shrinks to the space above the keyboard (280 ms); the sheet re-centers there and scrolls inside. The action row is sticky so Save stays reachable.
  • Full-page forms (Sign in email, First sign-in name and location, Weather setup, Settings › Location) pad the page by the keyboard height and scroll the focused field into view, 24 px below the top of the scroll area. Rows size to content, so the page scrolls instead of squeezing the hero.
  • Build with visualViewport resize (or interactive-widget=resizes-content), not fixed heights.
  • Hide-keyboard key or tapping outside a field closes it. Return in single-line fields moves to the next field; in Message it adds a line.
  • Idle timeout pauses while a field is focused.

Not designed anywhere

  • Session expiry rules (sign-ins don’t expire in v1), and kiosk lock (prevent leaving the app).
  • Multi-screen management and roles (who can change Settings).
  • Accessibility: focus order inside sheets. (Color swatches are named by season color, e.g. Amber, Rust, Sage, Coral, Cream, and use aria-pressed.)
