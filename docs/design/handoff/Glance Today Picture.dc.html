Glance · Today’s photo

The Today’s photo box (was “Picture of the day”) on Today at each screen size: large 1920×1080, normal 1024×768 (plus its 280 px minimum width at 769×768) and small 390×844. Each frame is the live mockup cropped to the box, so it matches Glance.dc.html exactly. Large and normal: bottom of the right column, under the notes box. Small: last block in the scrolling column, after the notes box.

Behaviour
Layout and size
  • Large and normal: bottom of the right column of the Today grid (min 280 px wide). The box is as tall as its content: the photo frame is a fixed 4:3, so the box never changes size with the note count. The notes box above takes the rest of the column.
  • Large: the photo frame is capped at 50% of the viewport height minus 64 px, so short windows keep room for notes. Small: no cap; last block in the scrolling column.
  • White canvas box, 24 px radius, 16 px padding, 12 px gap, the same as the notes box, so both eyebrows sit 24 px from the box edge.
Header
  • Eyebrow “Today’s photo”.
  • With a photo: ghost “Add photo” (image-plus), the same as the notes header. “All photos” (arrow-right) shows only when the account has one photo; otherwise the “{n} more photos” row at the bottom goes to Photos instead. Right column narrower than 440 px: 44 px white icon buttons with the same aria-labels.
  • No photos, first upload, loading, Add from your phone: no header buttons.
Photo
  • Photo in a 4:3 frame, object-fit cover, 8 px radius. object-position comes from the photo’s focal point (face detection on upload); with none, portrait photos sit at 50% 30% and landscape at the center.
  • Title 18 px / 480: one line with an ellipsis when the column is 440 px or wider, two lines below that. Then a 14 px mono caption: “On this day · {year}” for an anniversary photo, otherwise date · place.
  • Title fallback: the photo’s title, then its place, then its date. Camera file names (IMG_, DSC, PXL…) never show.
  • More photos: only when there are other photos, under the caption, a 52 px row → Photos view, the same as the notes box’s “{n} more notes” row. No fill: 1 px hairline divider on top, up to 3 overlapping 28 px round thumbnails of the other photos (2 px white ring, −8 px overlap), “{n} more photos” (17 px, 480, count of the other photos), arrow-right at the right edge; 0.97 press, aria-label “{n} more photos, open Photos”. Hidden when today’s photo is the only finished photo.
  • Photo, title and caption are one button: press scales to 0.97 (120 ms), aria-label “Today’s photo: {title}, {caption}”. Tap → Photos view with this photo featured, slideshow paused.
Which photo
  • One per calendar day, the same on every screen of the account; it changes at midnight in the location’s time zone with the 600 ms cross-fade.
  • First choice: a photo taken on today’s date in an earlier year. Otherwise the next in a shuffled cycle. Only finished uploads count.
  • Skips the photo that is the screen background, so the same photo never shows twice.
  • If today’s photo is deleted, the next in the cycle replaces it at once. Mock: today is Oct 9 2026, so it picks “Picnic on Sauvie Island” (Oct 9 2024).
Adding photos
  • Large (wider than 1200 px, the kiosk): “Add photo” and the empty tile open “Add from your phone” inside the box: a QR code on the hero block, “Scan to add photos”, plus “Choose files here” (secondary) and Done. Closes on Done, after the idle timeout, or when a photo arrives.
  • Normal and small: “Add photo” and the empty tile open the file picker. Uploads keep going in the background; progress shows in Photos.
States
  • No photos: one warm 8:7 tile (no border): stacked frames, “A memory to share?”, three example moments that change once a day (“A birthday cake, pizza night, the first day of school.”), “Add family photos and one shows here each day.” and a white pill (“Add from your phone” on large, “Add photos” on normal and small). aria-label is the pill label.
  • First upload: no finished photos yet but uploads running. The frame shows the first upload at 60% with the 8 px progress bar, “Your first photo is on its way” and a mono status (“Uploading · 64%”, “{n} photos · 64%”, “Waiting”, “Paused · offline”). Tap → Photos.
  • Loading: pulsing season-soft frame and two bars, no header buttons.
  • Didn’t load: soft frame, image-off icon, mono “Photo didn’t load”; title and caption stay. Retries on the next sync. Offline with no cached copy: “Shows when back online”. Offline with a cached copy: the photo shows as normal.
  • On photo background: the box stays a white panel and picks a different photo from the background.
