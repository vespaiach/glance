NotesCalendar behavior
Week strip
  • Phone (under 600 px). Replaced by a date control: year + month on the left, selected date pill with a chevron on the right. Tap opens the date picker (B): a vertical, scrollable list of days with month headers. Tap a day to select it and close.
  • Days. Seven day pills: weekday, date, one dot per person with a plan, and the forecast icon + high when the cell is at least 124 px wide. Selected = black; today keeps a 2 px ring.
  • Chevrons. Step a week. Landing on this week selects today; any other week selects its Sunday.
  • Dots. Always show everyone, whatever the filter.
Person filter
  • Row. One soft pill per member: color dot + name, light fill when not selected. Scrolls sideways when it doesn’t fit.
  • Default. All members selected = everyone. Every dot filled, nothing black.
  • Tapping. Tap a member while everyone shows → only that person (black pill). Tap more to add; tap a selected one to remove. Selecting every member returns to everyone.
  • Nobody. Removing the last one leaves nobody selected: all dots are hollow rings and the calendar is empty. It is not treated as everyone.
  • Scope. Filters the timeline, all-day tiles and free time. A plan shows if any selected person is on it.
  • Edit. Soft pill pinned after the last member, always in view. Opens the Family sheet (C).
Family
  • List. One row per member: color dot, name, plan count, Remove. The last member can’t be removed.
  • Remove. Inline confirm (D) says what happens: plans only for them are deleted, shared plans stay for the others.
  • Add. Name + a color no one uses yet, then Add. Empty → “Add a name”; duplicate → “Already in the family”. New members join plans that were for everyone.
  • Done. Changes apply right away; Done closes. A typed name not yet added asks to discard.
24 hours and Add event
  • 24 hours. Soft toggle pill. Off: 5 AM to 9 PM, with a black count badge when the day has plans outside that window. On: black pill, 12 AM to 12 AM; empty night hours are half height. Stays on across days.
  • Add event. Soft pill at the end of the row, same height as the filter. Creates on the selected day for the selected people.
Timeline
  • Fit. Always fits the card, no scrolling (phone and 24 hours get a taller minimum and the page scrolls).
  • Elastic hours. Busy hours grow, empty and past hours shrink. Not to scale on purpose. Reflows with a 280 ms ease.
  • Overlaps. Overlapping plans share the width in columns; every plan is at least 44 px tall.
  • Now. On today, a 2 px black line with a “10:41” pill in the gutter.
Plan cards
  • Content. Shows as much as the height allows: time + title, then who · where, then the first line of notes. Short cards become one line.
  • Color. One person → their color; several → season soft color with person dots; past → white, struck through; now → black ring + NOW pill.
  • Tap. Opens Event details; Edit to change. Past events are read-only.
Weather and free time
  • Weather lane. Right edge, aligned to the elastic hours: icon + temperature per hour, rain hours (≥ 50%) filled. Plans in rain get a “{p}% rain” chip. Only with a location and hourly forecast (today, tomorrow). Hidden on phone to save width.
  • Free slots. Gaps of 30 min or more between 5 AM and 9 PM show as dashed tiles (“Everyone free · 1 h 15 min”). Tap → New event in that slot.
  • All day. Soft tiles above the timeline.

  • who= Comma list (an, hy, mom, dad, thang, tin, nghe, an2, nga) or none.

  • dp=open opens the date picker (phone).
