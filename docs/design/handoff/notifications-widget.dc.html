Glance · event notifications

Reminders for upcoming events, shown on the display over any view and as a push notification on phones and tablets when Glance is closed. Each event fires 15 min before, at start, or both; the default comes from Settings. Notifications close by themselves; a tap opens the event. Events with the same reminder time merge into one. During quiet hours the reminder shows quietly on Ambient without waking the screen. The on-display notification is a compact black strip at the top center. Frames are the live mockup at 1920×1080, drawn at 40%. Logic and behavior: Notes · Notifications.
