Guide for families

Your family’s day, at a glance.

Glance turns a screen at home into one shared board for your family’s calendar, weather, notes and photos. This guide covers setup and the everyday basics.

01 · Sign in and account

Get started with one email

One family, one account, one email address. Every screen signed in with it shows the same calendar, notes and photos. There are no passwords.

  1. 1Open Glance on the screen, enter your family’s email and tap Send code.
  2. 2Type the 6-digit code from the email. It works once, for 10 minutes.
  3. 3New to Glance? Add your first name, then the city or ZIP where the screen is. That sets the weather and the clock’s time zone.

02 · Today

The whole day on one screen

  1. 1Now panel. The clock, today’s weather and what’s happening now or up next. Tap the weather or the event for more.
  2. 2Schedule. Today’s plans in time order. Tap a plan for details, or a dashed free slot to add an event then. Tap the faces at the top to show one person’s plans. Plans that have ended fold away; tap Earlier to see them.
  3. 3Notes and today’s photo. The newest family notes and one photo. Tap either to open it.
The menu hides after 15 seconds without a touch. Tap the white tab on the left edge to bring it back. After a few idle minutes, the screen returns to Today on its own.

03 · Calendar

Add an event

  1. 1Open Calendar from the menu and pick a day in the week strip. The arrows move a week at a time.
  2. 2Tap Add event, or tap a dashed free slot on the timeline to start at that time.
  3. 3The event reads as one sentence. Tap a word to change it: what, which day, start and end, who and where. Next moves to the next part.
  4. 4For a weekly series, such as soccer practice every Friday, tap the day and switch to Every week. The event repeats on that weekday from the day you picked, with no end date.
  5. 5Tap Save event. Each person’s plans show in their own color.
Tap any event to see its details, the forecast for that day and what else is on at that time. Tap Edit to change or delete it. For a repeating event, choose This event only or All events in the series. Events that have ended can’t be changed.

04 · Weather and location

Set where the screen is

Weather shows conditions now, the next 8 hours and a 7-day forecast. One place per family sets both the weather and the clock’s time zone.

  1. 1Go to Settings › Location.
  2. 2Type a city or ZIP code, or tap Use my location and choose Allow. US places only for now.
  3. 3If several places match, tap the right one. It saves straight away.

05 · Notes

Leave a note for the family

  1. 1Tap Add note on Today or on the Notes board.
  2. 2Write your message, up to 280 characters. Add your name under From if you like.
  3. 3Tap Post note. It shows on every screen, newest first.

Tap a note to read it in full. From there, Edit changes it and Delete removes it.

Notes clear themselves after 7 days. Change this, or turn it off, in Settings › Remove notes after.

06 · Photos

Share photos on the screen

  1. 1Add photos from a phone or tablet signed in to Glance: open Photos and tap Upload.
  2. 2Photos play as a slideshow. Use Play/Pause and the arrows, or tap a thumbnail to show it.
  3. 3Tap Set as background to put a photo behind every screen, or Edit details to fix its name, date or place.

Each family has 5 GB of photo space, shared by every screen. Settings › Account shows what’s left.

07 · Settings and seasons

Make it yours

Changes apply as soon as you tap. There is no Save button.

  • Season sets the screen’s colors. Auto follows the month: spring from March, summer from June, fall from September, winter from December.
  • Background shows a drawing for the current season unless you pick your own photo or a slideshow. Choose Blank for a plain background.
  • Clock and temperature: 12- or 24-hour, Fahrenheit or Celsius.

08 · Import, export and API

Bring events in and out

All three are at the bottom of Settings and need a connection.

  • Import events adds events from an .ics calendar file, up to 5 MB and 1,000 events. Send the file from your phone by scanning the code, or choose a file on this screen. Pick who the events are for, then tap Import. Events already on the calendar and past events are skipped. Imported events are a one-time copy: later changes to the file don’t show up. Undo removes the whole import for 24 hours.
  • Export events saves every upcoming event as an .ics file that other calendar apps can open. Past events are left out.
  • API key lets another app add and update events on your calendar. It can’t read or list events. Tap Create key and copy it right away; it’s shown only once. One key per account. Revoke it to stop the app, then create a new one if needed.

09 · Ambient

A calm screen for quiet hours

Ambient dims the screen to a large clock, the weather and the next event. Good for evenings and overnight.

  1. 1In Settings › Ambient screen, set the hours, for example 6:00 PM – 7:30 AM, and the brightness.
  2. 2To start it any time, tap the moon in the menu.
  3. 3Tap anywhere on the screen to wake it.

10 · Reminders

Never miss the next thing

  • Choose when reminders come in Settings › Notifications: 15 minutes before, at the start, both, or off.
  • A black strip appears at the top of every screen for 30 seconds. Tap it to see the event.
  • Phones and tablets with Glance closed get a push notification instead.
  • Turn the chime on or off in Settings. It never plays during quiet hours.

11 · Phone and tablet

Take Glance with you

Glance runs on phones and tablets too, with the same calendar, notes and photos. Add it to your home screen, then sign in one of two ways.

  1. 1On the big screen, open Settings › Account › Sign in another device. A code appears.
  2. 2On the phone, tap Scan code from another screen and point the camera at it. The code lasts 5 minutes.
  3. 3Or sign in with your family’s email and a code, as on the big screen.

Screen sizes

One app, any screen

Glance rearranges itself to fit the width of the screen. Nothing to set up.

Large Wider than 1200 px Wall and kiosk displays, TVs and laptops. Today fits in three columns with no scrolling.
Normal 769 – 1200 px Tablets and small laptops. Two columns: the Now panel and schedule first, then notes and the photo.
Small 768 px and below Phones. One scrolling column, and the menu opens from the edge.
