Glance · Today notes

The Today notes box at each screen size: large 1920×1080, normal 1024×768 (plus its 280 px minimum width at 769×768) and small 390×844. Each frame is the live mockup cropped to the box, so it matches Glance.dc.html exactly. Large and normal: top of the right column, above Today’s photo. Small: below the Today events box in the scrolling column. Clock reads 10:41 AM.

Behaviour
Layout and size
  • Large and normal: top of the right column of the Today grid (min 280 px). The box takes the column height left above Today’s photo (which keeps a fixed 4:3 photo frame); the “{n} more notes” row sits at the bottom and the no-notes tile fills the box.
  • Small: third block in the scrolling column, after the Now panel and the Today events box. Height fits its content.
  • White canvas box, 24 px radius, 16 px padding, the same as Today’s photo, so both eyebrows sit 24 px from the box edge.
Header
  • Eyebrow “Notes · {n}”, the total number of notes on the board. “Notes” while loading or with no notes.
  • Right column 440 px or wider: ghost “Add note” (square-pen) → New note and ghost “All notes” (arrow-right) → Notes view. Narrower: 44 px white icon buttons (square-pen, arrow-right) with those labels as aria-label. “All notes” is dropped when some notes are hidden; the “{n} more notes” row covers it. Today’s photo uses the same rule for “Add photo” and “All photos”.
  • No notes: no header buttons. Loading: “Add note” only.
Notes
  • Newest notes as small StickyNotes (150 px min height), text cut at 3 lines with an ellipsis; full text in Note details. One note per row, full box width, 20 px gap, at every screen size.
  • Large and normal: 2 notes when both fit in the box above Today’s photo, otherwise 1. Re-checked when the notes or the box width change. Small: up to 2.
  • Each note is a button: press scales to 0.97 (120 ms), aria-label “Note from {author}: {text}”. Tap → Note details.
  • Notes not shown: a 52 px row under the notes → Notes view. No fill: hairline divider, up to 3 overlapping 28 px paper-tone dots, “{n} more notes”, arrow-right at the right edge.
  • Color: every note is the season paper tone (cream; frost in winter), so color on Today only means a person. No color picker; tilt and shadow keep notes apart.
Variants
  • No notes: the whole dashed card (1.5 px hairline, 24 px radius) is one button → New note: a stack of three blank notes, “Something to share at home?”, one line of copy and an “Add note” pill label. Right column under 440 px: the stack is hidden so the card stays compact.
  • Loading: season-soft tiles shaped like the notes (150 px min height, 6 px radius, −2° and 1.5°), as many as the box will show, 20 px gap.
